Vice President of Engineering

BibliU is a UK remote, technology-led B2B company serving universities and
publishers. It is hiring a VP Engineering to lead and grow a roughly 15-person
engineering function across application and platform work.

The role owns team leadership, engineering standards, technical governance,
security posture, and delivery against the product roadmap. The first six months
focus on earning the team's trust, making delivery predictable, and establishing
practical AI-assisted development with suitable guardrails and review.

Must have:

- Significant VP, Director, or CTO-level software engineering leadership.
- A track record of leading teams through change.
- Delivery accountability for complex web and mobile applications, including release governance.
- Experience managing engineering teams and technical leads.
- Full-stack TypeScript and AWS experience.
- Ownership of application security, secure coding, vulnerability management, audits, and customer security review.

Good to have:

- Development governance, DevOps, CI/CD, and quality assurance standards.
- Cloud-native architecture and modern development frameworks.
- Practical AI-assisted development, including tooling, guardrails, review, and measurement.
- SaaS, EdTech, FinTech, or another product-led digital environment.
- International and multi-region deployments.
- Experience in a resource-constrained scale-up where sequencing matters.
