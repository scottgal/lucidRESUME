# lucidRESUME 2.6.5

This release improves evidence-based resume prose, job-specific summaries and
skills, and the native preview. It also fixes source formatting that prevented CI
from reaching tests on Linux, macOS and Windows.

## Resume generation

- Generates with available evidence while offering optional interviews for gaps.
- Retrieves reusable analysed prose from the definitive complete career, combines
  complementary role facts and preserves their original evidence links.
- Builds summaries from selected job-relevant accounts and filters the global
  skills list, reducing generic summaries and unrelated role tools.
- Preserves measurements and units, unknown outcomes, personal/team ownership,
  implementation scope and delivery status during model rewriting. Ambiguous
  edits retain accepted source prose rather than invent stronger achievements.
- Reuses validated prose accounts and carries specific rejection reasons into
  the existing repair pass, including cached responses.
- Adds repeatable synthetic prose evaluations and contrasting full-career job
  projections reviewed with Luna. These are regression checks, not a claim of
  perfect prose or general semantic entailment.

## Desktop and related evidence

- Wraps long preview bullets and their evidence links within the available width.
- Keeps Word/PDF output and source evidence available in the generated archive.
- Retains the analysed prose, blog discovery and related project workflows added
  since 2.6.0, with their native client regression coverage.

## Upgrade

Restart and generate a fresh resume to use the new selection and prose rules.
Archived versions retain their original content. Back up the complete application
data directory, including `data.db`, `generated-resumes/` and `prose-library/`.
macOS bundles require macOS 14 or later and are ad-hoc signed, not notarized.
See the [release guide](../release.md) for first-launch instructions.

The [prose research paper](../research/2026-10-12-evidence-preserving-prose.md)
records the quality loop, source-preserving fallbacks and evaluation limitations.
