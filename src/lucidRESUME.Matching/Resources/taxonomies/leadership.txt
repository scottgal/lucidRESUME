commercial judgement: commercial alignment, commercial strategy, commercial objectives, cost-conscious architecture
executive communication: executive stakeholder communication, board communication, vc pitch development
clear communication: stakeholder communication, client communication, supplier liaison
ownership: delivery ownership, release ownership, technical ownership, end-to-end ownership
operational resilience: production reliability, reliability engineering, disaster recovery, incident management
